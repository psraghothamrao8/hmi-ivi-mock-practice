What to learn from the eye test

  • Check things together, not alone (gauge + number, icon + text, toggle + label).
  • Numbers have limits: seconds ≤ 59, volume ≤ max, dates must exist.
  • Units must be consistent (km/h with km, °C with °C).
  • Language: everything translated, nothing cut off.
  • Time logic: ETA after now, trip ≤ odometer.
  • Colour rules: red = stop/danger, green = go/OK.
  • Always ask: "Does this make sense for a real driver?"
