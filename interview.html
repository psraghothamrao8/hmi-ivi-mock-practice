6. Interview Questions & Sample Answers

Important: These answers are models. Where you see [your example], use something that really happened in your internship. Use short sentences. Speak slowly. It is fine to say "let me think for a moment".

The answer formula (STAR)

SSituation
Where? Which project?
TTask
What was needed?
AAction
What did you do?
RResult
What happened?
If you don't know an answer, use this 3-step reply:
1️⃣ "I haven't worked on that directly." 2️⃣ "My understanding is … (say the basic idea)." 3️⃣ "I'd learn it quickly by … (docs, a senior colleague, hands-on practice)."
This sounds honest and confident. Never make up tools or experience.
💡 Many answers have a yellow "Remember" line – a short hook to recall the idea. 🎤 Practise speaking with the Mock Interview mode.

Questions YOU should ask them (shows ownership)

  1. What does the HMI test setup look like – real car, test bench, or simulation?
  2. Which tools do you use for signal simulation and test management?
  3. Which automation framework do you use, and what part would I run vs. maintain?
  4. How is the work split with the customer proxy team? How often do we talk?
  5. What would a successful first 3 months look like?
  6. How are defects reviewed – is there a daily triage?
  7. Is there training for the tools I haven't used yet?
