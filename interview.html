5. Interview Questions & Sample Answers

Important: The answers below are models. Where you see [your example], put something that really happened in your internship. Interviewers can tell when an answer is memorised or made up. Use short sentences. Speak slowly. It is fine to say "let me think for a moment".
Answer shape that always works (STAR): Situation (where) → Task (what was needed) → Action (what you did) → Result (what happened).

Questions YOU should ask them (shows ownership)

  1. What does the HMI test setup look like – real car, test bench, or simulation?
  2. Which tools are used for signal simulation and test management?
  3. Which automation framework do you use, and what part would I run vs. maintain?
  4. How is the work split with the customer proxy team? How often do we talk?
  5. What would a successful first 3 months look like?
  6. How are defects reviewed – daily triage?
  7. Is there training for the tools I haven't used yet?
